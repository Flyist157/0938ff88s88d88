    FAQ

    Answers to common questions from pros and customers.

    How is this different from Thumbtack?

    LeadPilot lets pros accept or decline every lead. You only pay after you accept, not just for receiving the lead.

    Can I set lead filters?

    Yes. Pros can control budget, service area, response windows, and job types so the inbox stays relevant.

    How do customers get matched?

    Customers submit a short request. The platform recommends pros who match the job and availability.

    Is there a monthly fee?

    No. There are no subscriptions required. Pros pay only when they accept a lead.
